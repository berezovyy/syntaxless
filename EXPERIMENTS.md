## Do not retry

- Hidden-size bump without new kinds (id 3).
- More samples/epochs on an unchanged corpus as the only change (id 2).
- Training on a gap/subset corpus (id 4).
- Copying `RESERVED_PHRASES` into `POSITIVE_CARRIERS`.
- Decoder regex / noun denylist / overlay restore.
- Promoting because gate floors (0.95 / 0.5% SMC) passed but reserved contracts are not 100%.
- Any train while `oracle:gate` is red.
